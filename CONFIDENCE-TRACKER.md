## What to Expect

**By Week 6:** Mostly 3s and 4s. You can build a React app. You understand closures. DSA no longer feels alien.

**By Week 10:** Mostly 4s and 5s. You have 2-3 deployed projects. You can explain your code in an interview.

**By Week 13:** Interview ready. Applying daily. Tracking applications.

**If any score stays at 1-2 for more than 2 consecutive weeks:** That topic needs dedicated attention — spend 30 extra minutes on it every day until it moves. A stuck score is a signal, not a verdict.

## The Score That Matters Most

The overall confidence score (1-10) in the last column. Not your JS score. Not your DSA score. The overall: "Do I believe I'm moving toward the goal?"

That number should trend upward over 13 weeks. It won't go up every week — some weeks are hard and the score drops. That's fine. What you're watching for is the trend over 4-5 weeks. If it's been flat or declining for a month, open `RESCUE-KIT.md`.
