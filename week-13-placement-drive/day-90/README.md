## Morning (8:00 – 11:00 AM) — Reflection

### Write in `journal.md`

Open `journal.md`. Write a reflection with these prompts. Do not rush this. Write honestly.

**Where I was 90 days ago:**
> What did you know? What couldn't you build? What were you afraid of?

**Where I am now:**
> What can you build? What have you shipped? What has become normal that used to be hard?

**What I'm proud of:**
> Be specific. Not "I worked hard." Name the exact thing. The day you got the Lighthouse score to 94. The first time a test you wrote caught a real bug. The first commit message that actually described what changed.

**What I struggled with:**
> Be honest. The days you didn't do the work. The concepts that still don't feel solid. The self-doubt that showed up. Name it.

**What I'd do differently:**
> If you started Day 1 again with what you know now, what would change? Be specific.

**What I'm going to do next:**
> The job hunt continues. What does your next 30 days look like?

## What Comes Next

The 90-day program is complete. The job hunt is not.

**Keep doing this every day until you're placed:**

- **Apply daily** — 5-10 applications every working day. Do not let this drop below 5.
- **Keep solving DSA** — 2-3 problems daily. Your skills will fade if you stop.
- **Keep building** — add features, contribute to open source, freelance if possible. Every new thing you ship is something to talk about in interviews.
- **Follow up** — on every application after 7 days, on every cold message after 5 days. One follow-up per contact, not more.
- **Debrief every interview** — win or lose, write what happened and what you'll do differently.
- **The first offer will come** — then open `job-hunt/salary-negotiation.md`. Read it before you respond. Always negotiate. Always.

**When you get placed:**

Come back here. Write the date in `journal.md`. Write which company. Write how it happened — was it a direct application? A referral? A cold message? A recruiter who found you on LinkedIn?

The story of how you got placed will help the next person who reads this.
