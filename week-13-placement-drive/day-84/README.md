## Afternoon (2:00 – 3:00 PM) — Cold Outreach

Today's outreach focus: engineers, not just recruiters. Find a senior frontend developer or tech lead at a company you applied to. Look at their LinkedIn — do they post? What do they work on? Send a message about their work specifically, not just "I applied to your company."

**Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I came across your post on [topic] — really resonated with your point about [specific thing].

I'm a frontend developer who's been deep in React for the past year. I applied for a role at [Company] and would love to learn more about the team's approach to [something from their stack/blog].

Portfolio if you're curious: [link]

Thanks, Dinesh
```
