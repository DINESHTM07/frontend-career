### What's working and what isn't

**If response rate is under 5%:** The problem is likely your resume or how you're filtering roles. Check: are you applying to roles that require 2+ years of experience? Are you using the same resume for every application? Is your resume ATS-friendly (no tables, no columns, standard section names)?

**If response rate is 5-10%:** This is normal for a fresher. Keep the volume up. Keep personalizing.

**If you have no callbacks at all after 30+ applications:** Something specific is wrong. Most likely options: resume doesn't show projects clearly enough, portfolio is hard to find in the application, LinkedIn profile doesn't match the resume. Fix the most likely one this weekend.
