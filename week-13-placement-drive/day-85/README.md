## IF YOU HAVE AN INTERVIEW CALLBACK — Do This Now

Open `interview-vault/` and prep specifically for that company.

**Company-specific prep checklist:**
- [ ] Read the company's "About" page, blog, and recent news
- [ ] Find the tech stack (LinkedIn, StackShare, job description)
- [ ] Practice the behavioral questions most relevant to their stage (startup vs enterprise vs product)
- [ ] Research the interviewer on LinkedIn — what have they worked on?
- [ ] Prepare 3 questions to ask them at the end of the interview
- [ ] Do a mock run of "tell me about yourself" tailored to this specific company
- [ ] Review your project that's most relevant to their domain

**Questions to prepare for:**
- "Why do you want to work at [Company]?" — have a real answer, not a generic one
- "What do you know about our product?" — show you've used it or researched it
- "Tell me about a project you built that's most similar to what we do"
