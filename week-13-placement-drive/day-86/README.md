## Morning (8:00 – 11:00 AM) — DSA + Interview Questions

### DSA

Create `day-86-dsa.js`. Focus: linked list problems (reverse a linked list, detect cycle, find middle node). These show up in mid-level frontend interviews and are often the "harder" problem in a two-problem screen.

### Interview Questions

10 questions — today focus on behavioral and "tell me about yourself". Say your intro out loud right now. Time it. If it's over 90 seconds or includes filler words ("um", "like", "basically"), fix it.

## Follow-Up Block (2:00 – 3:00 PM) — Outreach + Follow-Ups

### Follow up on Day 83 applications

Open `application-tracker.md`. Find every application from Day 83 that still shows status "Applied" with no response. Send a follow-up message to the hiring manager or recruiter on LinkedIn:

**Follow-up message:**
```
Hi [Name],

I applied for the Frontend Developer role at [Company] on [date].
I'm very interested in this opportunity and wanted to follow up.

I recently [something you built or improved this week — keep it brief].

Happy to share more if helpful. Portfolio: [link]

Thanks, Dinesh
```

One follow-up per application. Do not send two. Mark "Follow-up sent" in tracker.

### Cold outreach

3-5 new messages. Today focus: companies where you found a specific engineering blog post or open source project you can mention.
