## Morning (8:00 – 11:00 AM) — DSA + Interview Questions

### DSA (3-4 problems, 20 min timer each)

Create `day-83-dsa.js`. Focus this week on two pointers, sliding window, and binary search — these appear most in frontend technical screens.

Rules:
- Timer on. 20 minutes per problem.
- State your approach out loud before writing code.
- State time and space complexity after solving.
- If not solved in 20 min: read the solution, understand it, then solve again from scratch.

### Interview Questions (10 questions out loud)

Open any file in `interview-vault/`. Pick 10 questions marked ⚠️ or ❌ from Week 12. Read → close → explain out loud → compare. This block only counts if you speak.

## Application Block (11:00 AM – 1:00 PM) — Apply to Jobs

### Step 1 — Open your target list

Open `job-hunt/company-research.md`. Start with **Tier 1 companies**. Go to each career page and search for: "Frontend Developer", "React Developer", "Frontend Engineer", "UI Developer".

### Step 2 — Apply with intention

For each application:
1. Read the job description fully
2. Check your resume matches at least 60% of the requirements
3. Customize your cover letter if the platform allows (2-3 sentences — why this company specifically)
4. Submit
5. Log it immediately in `job-hunt/application-tracker.md`

**What to log:**
```
| Company | Role | Date Applied | Platform | Status | Notes |
```

**Platforms today:** Company career pages (direct — highest quality), LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound, Instahyre, Cutshort.

**Volume target:** At least 5. Aim for 10. Max 15 minutes per application — move fast, apply, log, next.

---

## Afternoon (2:00 – 3:00 PM) — Cold Outreach

Open `job-hunt/cold-email-templates.md`. Send 3-5 LinkedIn messages to hiring managers or frontend engineers at companies you just applied to.

**What a good message looks like:**
```
Hi [Name],

I just applied for the Frontend Developer role at [Company]. I've been
building with React for the past year and recently shipped [specific project].

Your team's work on [something specific] caught my attention.

Would you be open to a quick chat? Portfolio: [link]

Thanks, Dinesh
```

Short. Specific. Has a call to action. Mentions something real about their work.

Log every message in `application-tracker.md` under "Outreach".
