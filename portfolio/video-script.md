## Recording Tips

### Setup

- **Camera:** Eye level — prop your laptop on books if needed. Looking slightly up into a camera is worse than looking slightly down.
- **Lighting:** One strong light source in *front* of you (window, ring light, lamp). Never record with a window behind you — it silhouettes your face.
- **Background:** Plain wall, bookshelf, or a clean desk. The less visual noise, the more the viewer focuses on you.
- **Framing:** Head and shoulders in frame. Don't fill the entire frame with your face — leave a little space above your head.
- **Audio:** Built-in laptop mic is fine if you're in a quiet room. Close windows, turn off fans. Record a 10-second test first and listen back on headphones.

### Delivery

- **Look at the camera lens** — not at your own face on screen. Tape a small sticky note arrow pointing at the lens if it helps.
- **Speak 20% slower than feels natural.** When you're nervous, your brain says "this is too slow." It's not. It's right.
- **Pause between sections.** A half-second pause after each section change feels like confident breathing on camera. It sounds like a gap in your head.
- **Smile at the start and end.** Not throughout — just the greeting and the final "let's talk." It changes the energy of the whole video.
- **Do 3–5 takes minimum.** The first take is always stiff. Take 3 or 4 is usually the one you use.
- **Don't apologize in the recording** ("um, sorry let me restart"). Stop, breathe, and do the whole thing again clean.

### After Recording

- Watch it back on mute first — does your body language look confident?
- Watch it back with audio only — does every sentence land, or do you trail off?
- Trim the first and last 0.5 seconds. Most recordings have an awkward freeze at the start and end.
- Add subtitles if you're posting to LinkedIn — most people watch without audio.

### Where to Post

- **LinkedIn** — native video post performs better than a YouTube link. Keep it under 3 minutes (you're under 2 — perfect).
- **Portfolio** — embed or link in your contact/hero section.
- **Job applications** — paste the LinkedIn video URL in your cover letter or email intro.
- **GitHub profile** — link in your README.
