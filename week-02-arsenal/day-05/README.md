## HOW TO WORK THROUGH THESE

1. **Open the file.** Read the INTRO and MENTAL MODEL sections first.
2. **Run the existing code.** Understand what it does before changing it.
3. **Answer the CHECKPOINT questions** before moving to YOUR TURN.
4. **Build YOUR TURN from scratch.** Don't scroll to see a solution — there isn't one.
5. **Move on only when YOUR TURN works.** Not when it "seems right" — when it runs.

> "Understanding the example is reading. Building YOUR TURN is learning."
