**Remote-first strategy:**
- Build a strong GitHub profile before applying to Toptal/Turing/Arc — they evaluate code, not just resumes
- "Remote" on LinkedIn means different things — filter by "Remote" AND check the job description for "India" or "IST"
- Wellfound is the best single source for funded Indian startups with remote options
- Post your portfolio on LinkedIn as a native video + article — inbound beats outbound for remote

## Application Priority Matrix

Use this to decide where to spend your time each week:

```
HIGH EFFORT, HIGH REWARD (spend 60% of time here)
→ Tier 1 startups (Razorpay, Chargebee, Freshworks, Zoho, Groww)
→ Tier 4 remote platforms (Wellfound, LinkedIn with proper filtering)

LOWER EFFORT, DECENT REWARD (spend 25% of time here)
→ Tier 2 Chennai product companies (Kissflow, Kovai, Facilio)
→ Tier 3 via referrals (not cold portal applications)

BACKGROUND APPLICATIONS (spend 15% of time here)
→ Tier 3 portals (TCS, Infosys, Wipro — apply once, don't chase)
→ Freelance platforms (Contra, Gun.io — build profile while job hunting)
```

## Weekly Research Ritual (30 min every Monday)

1. Check Wellfound for new React/Frontend roles posted in the last 7 days
2. Check LinkedIn Jobs — sort by "Most Recent", filter React + India/Remote
3. Check Hirist and Instahyre for product company listings
4. Visit the careers pages of your top 5 Tier 1 targets directly
5. Note any new roles in `application-tracker.md`
