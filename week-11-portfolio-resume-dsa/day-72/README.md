## Morning (8:00 – 11:00 AM) — Write and Practice the Script

### Step 1 — Write the Script

Open `portfolio/video-script.md`. Write a 90-second script (roughly 200-225 words when spoken at a natural pace).

**Structure (stick to this):**

```
[0:00 – 0:10] — Hook: who you are in one sentence
[0:10 – 0:35] — What you built: mention all 3 projects by name
[0:35 – 1:05] — Your technical approach: 2-3 things you do well
[1:05 – 1:20] — What you're looking for
[1:20 – 1:30] — Call to action: how to reach you
```

**Example script (adapt this to your own voice — don't read it verbatim):**

```
Hi, I'm Dinesh — a frontend developer based in [City].

In the last 90 days I built three deployed projects from scratch.
First, an e-commerce app with React, Zustand, and React Query —
search, filter, sort, cart, favorites, dark mode, all working together.
Second, an analytics dashboard with four Recharts chart types,
TypeScript throughout, and a CSV export feature.
Third, a Next.js application using App Router, Server Components,
Shadcn/UI, and the Metadata API for SEO.

I care about the details that matter: type safety, accessible components,
loading states that actually appear on slow connections, and tests
that catch real bugs. I also submitted my first open source PR this month.

I'm looking for a junior frontend role where I can keep building and
growing with a team that takes their craft seriously.

If that sounds interesting, my portfolio has everything — live demos,
GitHub repos, and the resume. I'd love to talk.
```

**Rules for the script:**
- No "um", "like", or filler words — practice them out
- Speak slower than you think you need to — video makes people talk too fast
- No apologies — "I'm just a junior" or "I don't have much experience" — cut these
- Specific project names and technologies, not generic descriptions
- End with a clear action: "check the portfolio" or "let's talk"

### Step 2 — Practice Out Loud

Before recording:
1. Read the script out loud 3 times without stopping
2. Time yourself — is it under 90 seconds? Trim if not.
3. Practice once without looking at the script
4. Practice once while looking at the camera (your laptop camera or phone)

## Midday (11:20 AM – 1:30 PM) — Record + Select + Upload

### Recording Setup

**Camera:** Your laptop webcam is fine. Your phone camera propped up is better.

**Lighting:**
- Face a window (not a window behind you — that makes you a silhouette)
- If recording at night: a desk lamp in front of you at face height
- No overhead-only lighting (creates unflattering shadows)

**Background:**
- Plain wall or a simple desk setup — no clutter visible
- If your room is messy: record from the neck up with the camera close

**Audio:**
- Record in a quiet room — no AC, fan, or TV audible
- Earbuds work well as a microphone
- Do a 10-second test recording first — listen back before recording the full take

### Recording Takes

Record at least 3 takes. Rules:
- Don't stop mid-take if you stumble — keep going, cut it in editing
- After each take, watch it back immediately — note what to improve
- Take 1: get comfortable with the format
- Take 2: focus on eye contact with the camera (not the screen)
- Take 3: focus on pace and energy — speak as if you're excited, not nervous

### Selecting the Best Take

Watch all takes and choose the one where:
- You sound natural (not read-from-a-script)
- You maintain eye contact with the camera
- Your pace is comfortable — not rushed
- No significant stumbles or long pauses

### Trimming (Optional)

If any take needs a trim at the start or end:
- On Windows: Photos app → Edit → Trim
- On Mac: QuickTime → Edit → Trim
- Free online: Clipchamp, CapCut

### Upload and Link

Upload options:
1. **YouTube (unlisted)** — paste the link on your portfolio. Unlisted = only people with the link can watch.
2. **Loom** — free, easy to embed, gives you a short link
3. **Vimeo** — better player quality than YouTube, clean embed

After uploading:
- Add the video link to your portfolio (in the hero section or About section)
- Update `portfolio/video-script.md` with the final video URL

## Afternoon (1:30 – 3:30 PM) — DSA (8 Problems — Intensive)

Today's DSA block is 8 problems — following the curriculum brief for Day 73. Time yourself: aim for 15-20 minutes per problem. This simulates the pressure of an interview.

Create `day-72-dsa.js` in this folder.

For each problem:
- Start a timer before reading the problem
- Stop the timer when you submit a passing solution
- Write the time taken in a comment
- Write the time complexity and key insight

Problems to focus on: arrays, strings, hash maps — the most common interview category.

**Explain your approach out loud while you code.** Even alone. This is the skill interviews actually test — not whether you can solve it, but whether you can explain your thinking while solving it.
