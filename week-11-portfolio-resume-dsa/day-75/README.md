## Morning (8:00 – 11:00 AM) — Wellfound + Instahyre

### Platform 1 — Wellfound (formerly AngelList)

Wellfound is the best platform for startup jobs. Companies here often move faster and care more about what you've built than where you studied.

**Go to wellfound.com → sign in with your account from yesterday.**

Fill these sections completely:

**Name + Location:** Your real name, your city. If open to remote, add "Open to Remote."

**Headline:** Same as your LinkedIn headline.
```
Frontend Developer | React · TypeScript · Next.js | Open to Junior Roles
```

**About / Bio:** Same as your LinkedIn About section (3 paragraphs).

**Skills:** Add all technical skills — React, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, Git, Vitest, Zustand, REST APIs.

**Projects:** Add all 3 with live URLs. Wellfound lets you add a URL per project — use your live Vercel URLs.

**Job Preferences:**
- Job Type: Full-time
- Desired Salary: Research the range for junior frontend in your market (use levels.fyi or Glassdoor). Enter a range, not a single number.
- Work Authorization: Your actual status
- Remote: Yes (if applicable)
- Locations: Your city + Remote

**Profile completeness:** Aim for 100%. Wellfound shows your completion percentage — don't leave it at 70%.

### Platform 2 — Instahyre

Instahyre is strong for India-based tech companies. Recruiters here search by skills and tech stack.

**Go to instahyre.com → sign in.**

Fill:
- Profile photo (same professional photo as LinkedIn)
- Headline: "Frontend Developer | React | TypeScript | Next.js"
- Skills: Add all skills — Instahyre matches you to jobs by skills, so be thorough
- Projects: Add all 3 with descriptions and live URLs
- Resume: Upload `Dinesh_S_Frontend_Developer_Resume.pdf`
- Experience level: Fresher or 0-1 years
- Expected salary: the range you researched

**Set up job alerts:** Go to "Job Alerts" or "Email Preferences" → set alert for "Frontend Developer" + your skills + your location → daily frequency.

## Midday (11:20 AM – 1:30 PM) — Cutshort + Hirect + Naukri

### Platform 3 — Cutshort

Cutshort uses AI to match candidates to companies. The more complete your profile, the better the matches.

**Go to cutshort.io → sign in.**

Fill:
- Skills: be thorough — Cutshort weighs these heavily in matching
- Projects: add all 3 with GitHub + live URLs
- Bio: paste from LinkedIn
- Upload resume PDF
- Set job preferences: "Frontend Developer", Junior level, Full-time + Remote

Enable email notifications for new matches.

### Platform 4 — Hirect

Hirect is unique — it connects you directly with founders and CTOs via chat. Small companies hire quickly here.

**Go to hirect.in → sign in.**

Fill:
- Profile completely — photo, skills, about, projects
- Set your target role and salary
- Enable "Available for opportunities"

On Hirect, you can also proactively browse companies and message them directly. Today just set up the profile — outreach starts in Week 12.

### Platform 5 — Naukri

Naukri is the largest job board in India. Volume is high here — you need to be visible.

**Go to naukri.com → sign in.**

Fill:
- Complete profile (Naukri shows a "Profile Score" — aim for 90+)
- Upload your resume PDF
- Skills: add all technical skills
- Projects: add all 3
- Desired role: "Frontend Developer" / "React Developer" / "JavaScript Developer"
- Notice period: "Immediately available"

**Set job alerts:** My Naukri → Job Alerts → "Frontend Developer" → your location + Remote → daily digest.

**Resume visibility:** Set to "Anyone can view" — not private.
