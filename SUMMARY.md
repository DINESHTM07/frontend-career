## How to Navigate This Repo

**Starting fresh?** → Read `LEARNING-GUIDE.md` first.

**Studying for interviews?** → Open `interview-vault/` daily.

**Building a project?** → Use `project-starters/`, then add to `portfolio/src/data/projects.js`.

**Job hunting?** → `job-hunt/` has everything — companies, emails, tracker, negotiation.

**Posting on LinkedIn?** → `linkedin-post-drafts/` has 5 ready drafts. Post in order.

**Stuck?** → `LEARNING-GUIDE.md` → "What To Do When You Feel Stuck"
